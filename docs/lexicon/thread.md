# Thread

- **Tags:** gameplay, design
- **Status:** ✅ (2026-10-04)
- **Journal:** unlocks in One Pot of Red, whose cloth is a single thread; page on the Loom tab
- **Also called:** row (across), column (down); weft and warp (the loom's words)

## Gameplay

A thread is one line of stitches in a cloth: a row across, or a column down. The paint box's cloths are single threads.

## Design

- Across: one row, the weft, woven left to right in one pass of the shuttle. Down: one column, a warp thread (DESIGN.md §4).
- The paint box's levels each weave one thread of 8 stitches, and the threads stack into its quilt. Opposites and Turn the Wheel wove one thread of every paint in journal order until the chapter plan (2026-10-05) made them 8×6 swatches that sit in chapter 2's quilt as full patches; their first row is still that thread.
- No longer the word for what joins pieces: that's a [tube](tube.md).

## Implementation

No type of its own: a thread level is a level whose target picture is one row.

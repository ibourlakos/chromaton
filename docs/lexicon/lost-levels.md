# Lost Levels

- **Tags:** design
- **Status:** ✅ the idea and the name (designer, 2026-10-05); ❓ how players find it
- **Also called:** custom campaign, hidden campaign

## Design

- A hidden extra campaign for levels that leave the main campaign. Levels aren't deleted unless they make no sense at all; one that no longer fits a chapter (a repeat, a spike, a chapter over 12 levels) goes here instead (DESIGN.md §5.1, campaign rules).
- Its line under the chapter name (designer, 2026-10-05): "Levels that wandered off the campaign. They still weave." Its levels so far: Flip Side and The Harbour (their text in levels/level-text.md).
- Open: how it's found, its order, whether it has a quilt. Once players can see it, it becomes a gameplay term with a Gameplay section and a Journal line.

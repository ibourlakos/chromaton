# Brown

- **Tags:** gameplay, design
- **Status:** ✅ the honourable mention (designer, 2026-10-18; idea from playtest R3, 2026-10-06)
- **Journal:** unlocks in The Black Pot; page on the Paint tab

## Gameplay

In a real paint box, red, yellow and blue stirred together make a muddy brown. Here they make black, so brown gave its place to black. It didn't go far: it's the wood of every tub, bench and shelf in the workshop.

## Design

- Brown is not one of the eight paints. In a real paint box, red, yellow and blue mixed together make brown; in Chromaton the same three make [black](paint.md). So brown is truly related to the three basic paints, and it gave its place to black.
- Brown's hues dress the interface instead: the wood of the critters and benches, the ink, the stamp brown of the top bar (`WOOD`, `WOOD_DK`, `WOOD_LT`, `INK` in `ui/palette.gd`). They are neutrals on the paper background, so they never collide with the eight reserved signal hues (DESIGN.md §7.1).
- The honourable mention is a word on the journal's Paint tab (and so on Words), unlocking in The Black Pot, the level where every paint together makes black: the moment brown lost its place. Its picture is a wooden plank with a black drop on it, so the word is drawn in wood, never as a brown paint drop.
- It is not a paint: no drop, no glyph dots, no pot, and no level ever weaves it.
